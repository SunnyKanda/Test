Terms of Service

Last updated: 22 May 2026

These simple terms apply to odd-job work carried out by Handy Halifax. They’re here so both sides know what to expect.

Quotes and pricing

Prices on the website are guide prices — starting points for straightforward jobs. The final price depends on job size, wall type, access, materials, fixings and the number of items. We will always confirm the price with you before starting work.

What we take on

We only carry out small, straightforward odd jobs and easy DIY tasks. We do not carry out major building work, gas work, certified electrical work, complex plumbing, roofing, structural work, large waste removal or specialist trade jobs. If a job isn’t suitable for us, we’ll tell you.

Booking and access

Please make sure the work area is accessible and that we can reach the items involved. If we can’t carry out the job because of access, missing parts or fixings, we’ll discuss the options with you.

Materials and fixings

We bring standard tools for normal odd jobs. Specialist fixings, brackets or parts may need to be supplied by you or added to the quote.

Payment

Payment is due on completion of the work unless agreed otherwise. We confirm the agreed price before starting.

Cancellations

If you need to cancel or rearrange, please let us know as early as you can so we can offer the slot to someone else.
