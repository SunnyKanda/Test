## Description

> ਨਿੱਕੇ ਬੋਲ (Nikke Bol) means “little words”. It's a warm, playful way for children and families to learn Punjabi, from a baby's first words to reading and writing Gurmukhi.
>
> **Hear every word in Punjabi.** Over 700 words, letters, syllables and sentences are recorded into the app. No setup, no internet needed.
>
> **A learning path.** 38 short units take learners from “ਸਤ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਅਕਾਲ” to family words, colours, numbers, animals, food, days and months, with a quick quiz and up to three stars for each.
>
> **Read and write Gurmukhi.** Learn the 35 letters of the ਪੈਂਤੀ, then every vowel sign (ਲਗਾਂ ਮਾਤਰਾਂ) one at a time, the full ਬਾਰਾਂਖੜੀ sound chart, and trace letters with a finger.
>
> **Speak Punjabi.** Build sentences like “ਇਹ ਨੀਲੀ ਗੱਡੀ ਹੈ” and see why words change, then record yourself and compare with the app's voice.
>
> **Made for families.**
> • A profile for each child, with their own path, streak and progress
> • Daily goals, streaks and a practice calendar for grown-ups
> • Record Nani's or Dadi's voice and share it with the whole family
> • Baby, Child and Grown-up learner levels
>
> **Safe for kids.** No ads. No in-app purchases. No accounts. Nothing is collected or tracked; everything stays on your device. Settings are behind a grown-ups check.

## Categories and age

- **App Store:** Primary *Education*, secondary *Kids*. Kids Category age band: **Ages 5 and Under** (or 6–8; the path suits 5–8, the baby level suits under 5). Age rating questionnaire: all “None” → **4+**.
- **Google Play:** Category *Education*. Target audience: **Ages 5 and under, 6–8, and 13+ (parents)**. Join *Designed for Families*. Content rating questionnaire (IARC): Education, no violence, no user interaction, no data sharing → **Everyone / PEGI 3**.

## Privacy answers

- **App Store privacy “nutrition label”:** Data Not Collected.
- **Google Play Data safety:** No data collected. No data shared. Data is not encrypted in transit because none is sent. Users can delete data by deleting a learner or the app.
- **Privacy policy URL:** host `nikke-bol/privacy.html` (for example on GitHub Pages) and paste its address. Fill in the contact email at the bottom first.
- **Microphone:** used only when the user taps record; explained in the permission prompt.
