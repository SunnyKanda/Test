Privacy policy

Nikke Bol (ਨਿੱਕੇ ਬੋਲ) · Last updated 24 October 2026

Nikke Bol is a Punjabi learning app for children and families. We built it so that nothing about you or your child leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking. We do not collect names, email addresses, location, contacts or device identifiers.

What stays on your device

  • Learner names and pictures you type in, so each child has their own progress.
  • Progress: words practised, review schedule, learning path stars, minutes practised each day and streaks.
  • Settings such as level, daily goal and speaking speed.
  • Family recordings, if a grown-up chooses to record their own voice.

This is stored in the app's own storage on your phone or tablet. Deleting the app deletes it. You can delete a learner and their progress at any time from the grown-ups area.

Microphone

The microphone is only used when you press a record button: to record a family voice for a word, or to compare your own pronunciation. Recordings are saved on your device and are never uploaded by the app.

Backups and voice packs

If a grown-up chooses “Save a backup” or “Save a voice pack”, the app creates a file and hands it to your device's share sheet. Where that file goes (for example, your own cloud storage or a message to a family member) is your choice.

Children

The app is designed for children under 13 together with their parents. Settings, sharing and deleting are behind a grown-ups check. There are no links out of the app in the children's areas, and no in-app purchases.
