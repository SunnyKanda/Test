Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 May 2026

This page explains what personal information Handy Halifax (“we”, “us”) collects when you ask for a quote, and how we use it. We keep this short and plain because we only collect what we need to do the job.

What we collect

When you use the quote form or contact us, you may give us:

  • Your name and phone number
  • Your postcode and preferred day/time
  • Details of the job and any photos you choose to send

How we use it

  • To prepare your quote and arrange the work
  • To contact you about your enquiry, usually by phone or WhatsApp

We do not sell your details, and we do not use them for marketing without your agreement.

Who can see it

If you contact us through WhatsApp, your message is handled under WhatsApp’s own privacy terms. If the quote form’s backup feature is enabled, a copy of your enquiry is sent to our form provider purely so we receive it reliably. We do not share your details with anyone else.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as needed to quote and complete the work, plus a reasonable period for our records, then we delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Just contact us using the details below.
